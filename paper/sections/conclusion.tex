\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented the first systematic characterisation of induction circuit stability
under fine-tuning in two-layer attention-only transformers.
By measuring per-head induction scores and activation-patching attribution at every
100-step checkpoint across both a code and a prose condition, we provide a replicable
methodology for circuit-level monitoring during fine-tuning.

\textbf{[SUMMARY PENDING EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS]}

The interactive visualisation dashboard and full codebase are released alongside this
paper, enabling practitioners to apply circuit-level monitoring to their own
fine-tuning pipelines.

\textbf{Future work.}
(1)~Extend to GPT-2 small (with MLP layers) using automated circuit
discovery~\citep{conmy2023automated}.
(2)~Investigate circuit-targeted regularisation during fine-tuning to preserve
induction circuit integrity while maintaining task performance.
