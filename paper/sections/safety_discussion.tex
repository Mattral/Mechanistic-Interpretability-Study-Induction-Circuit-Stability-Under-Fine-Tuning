\section{Safety Discussion}
\label{sec:safety}

The induction circuit is fundamental to in-context learning~\citep{olsson2022context}.
If fine-tuning on a narrow distribution degrades this circuit, deployed fine-tuned
models may exhibit reduced in-context generalisation outside their target domain---a
property standard capability benchmarks do not measure.

\textbf{Implication for fine-tuning safety evaluations:} Our results
(\textbf{pending}) suggest induction scores should be included alongside capability
benchmarks when evaluating fine-tuned models.
A model that passes benchmarks but shows degraded induction scores may be less robust
to novel in-context prompts encountered in deployment.

Additionally, if phase transitions in circuit metrics occur during fine-tuning,
standard early-stopping criteria based on validation loss may not capture circuit
degradation---a training run can lower loss while simultaneously restructuring safety-
relevant computational mechanisms.
